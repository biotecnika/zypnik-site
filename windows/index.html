Zypnik for Windows

Hold Ctrl + Win, speak, let go. Neatly written text appears wherever you're typing: email, WhatsApp, Word, Slack, your browser.

Download Zypnik for Windows

Windows 10 and 11 · about 2 MB · no admin rights needed

Install in a minute

  1. Click Download above and open Zypnik.exe.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's because Zypnik is new. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.
  3. Zypnik installs itself and opens a welcome window. Click Continue with Google and sign in with your work account.
  4. That's it. Zypnik sits in the tray by the clock and starts with Windows.
If your antivirus asks about Zypnik, allow it: Zypnik listens for its shortcut keys so it knows when you want to talk. It doesn't record or send anything until you hold the shortcut.

How to use it

Talk
Click where you want to type, hold Ctrl + Win, speak, let go.
Long messages
Ctrl + Win + Space for hands-free. Press Ctrl + Win again to finish.
Cancel
Press Esc or click ✕ on the violet pill.
Names spelled right
Tray icon → Dictionary. Same list as the Zypnik phone app.

Prefer another key? Tray icon → Talk shortcut → Hold Right Alt.

Private by design

The microphone is used only while you hold the shortcut. Your recording is sent to Zypnik's speech service to be written out, then discarded. Zypnik doesn't read your screen on Windows. Privacy policy
