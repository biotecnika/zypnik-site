Delete your Zypnik account

Zypnik by Hyperlinkers (BioTecNika Info Labs Pvt. Ltd.)

Fastest: from the app

  • Android: open Zypnik → Settings tab → Delete my account → confirm.
  • Windows: click the Zypnik icon by the clock → Delete my account… → OK.

Without the app

Email hyperlinkers@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Zypnik account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (Google email and account ID)
  • Your Zypnik dictionary
  • Your voice-typing minutes record

Voice recordings and the text made from them are never stored, so there is nothing else to delete. Feedback you sent is kept without your name or email. Settings on your own phone or PC are removed when you uninstall the app.
