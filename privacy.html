Zypnik — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Zypnik ("the app", made by the Hyperlinkers team) is published by BioTecNika Info Labs Pvt. Ltd., Bengaluru, India ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Zypnik Android app, Zypnik for Windows and the zypnik.com website.

The short version

  • We don't sell personal data, and there is no advertising.
  • Screen text you highlight is read on your phone and never leaves it.
  • Zypnik reads the screen only when you use the Zypnik tab, and only the part you swipe.
  • Your voice is sent to our servers only while you use voice typing, to be turned into text. We don't keep the recording or the text.
  • You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, at any time from the app or at zypnik.com/delete-account.

1. Highlighting text on the screen (Android)

Zypnik shows a small tab at the edge of the screen. When you tap it and swipe or circle part of the screen, the app finds links, phone numbers, email addresses, codes and QR codes in that part, so you can open, call, write or copy them. To do this it uses Android's Accessibility Service:

  • At the moment you swipe, it reads the text of the area you highlighted.
  • When you tap the tab, it takes one still picture of the screen so moving content pauses while you highlight. Only the part you highlight is read, on your phone, using on-device recognition (Google ML Kit, built into the app). The picture is deleted straight after.
  • Screenshots you share to Zypnik, and text you send from the text-selection menu, are read the same way, on your phone, and not stored.
  • It notes which app is on screen and whether a keyboard is open, only to show the tab in the apps you chose and to offer voice typing while you type.
  • In banking, payment and investing apps, Zypnik hides its tab and reads nothing.

Zypnik never reads anything between swipes, never records your screen, never reads password fields, and never taps or changes anything in other apps, except typing your spoken words into the box you chose. None of this screen content is sent to us.

2. Voice typing

Cloud voice typing (the default when you're signed in, on Android and Windows): while you speak, the app records your voice and sends the recording to our server (Supabase). The server passes it to a speech-to-text provider (Sarvam AI in India, or Deepgram, Groq or OpenAI) and an AI editing step (Groq) that fixes grammar, punctuation and formatting, then sends the text back. Depending on where you are, this processing happens on servers in India, the United States or Europe. With live voice typing the audio is streamed while you speak, so the text is ready sooner. We don't keep the recording or the text.

To help spell names correctly, the app also sends the words in your Zypnik dictionary and the name of the app you're typing into (for example "WhatsApp"), so the text can be formatted for it.

On-phone voice typing (Android): if you aren't signed in, or choose on-phone speech, Android's own speech recognition turns your speech into text. When you're signed in, that text is sent to our server only for the AI editing step, and isn't kept.

The microphone is used only from the moment you start voice typing until you stop or cancel.

These providers process the audio and text on our behalf under their API terms. Groq and OpenAI don't use API data to train their models, and we switch off Deepgram's model-improvement programme for our requests.

3. Your account

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, we store:

  • your Google account email and an account ID;
  • how many seconds of cloud voice typing you used each month (for your free minutes);
  • your Zypnik dictionary (names and words you added), so it's the same on your phone and PC;
  • your snippets (short phrases you set up to expand into longer text), speech-memory words, and your writing-style settings (tone, clean-up level and language for each type of app);
  • simple usage counts, such as how many words and dictations you've made, to show your stats. These are numbers only, not your text.

Account data is stored with Supabase in India (Mumbai region).

4. Feedback

If you use "Send feedback", we receive your message, the app version, your device model and system version and, if you're signed in, your email, so we can reply and fix problems.

5. What is stored on your device

Your settings (which apps show the tab, which side it sits on, your talk shortcut on Windows and similar), your dictionary and, if you sign in, your sign-in session. On Windows, if you leave history on, your recent dictations are kept on your PC only, encrypted for your Windows user, and you can clear them at any time. On Windows the session is encrypted for your Windows user. Uninstalling the app removes these.

6. What is shared

Nothing you highlight. We use no analytics or advertising tools. Voice recordings and text go to our speech and AI providers only to produce your text, as described above. When you tap an action, the app passes the link, number or email to the app you choose (your browser, dialer, email app or WhatsApp), which handles it under its own privacy policy.

7. Deleting your data

  • In the Android app: Settings tab → Delete my account.
  • On Windows: tray icon → Delete my account…
  • Without the app: see zypnik.com/delete-account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, dictionary and usage record from our servers straight away. Feedback you sent is kept without your name or email.

8. Children

Zypnik is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
