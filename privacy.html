Zypnik — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Zypnik ("the app", made by the Hyperlinkers team) is published by BioTecNika Info Labs Pvt. Ltd., Bengaluru, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

The short version

  • Zypnik does not sell any personal data and has no advertising.
  • Everything you highlight is read on your phone and never leaves it.
  • It reads screen content only when you use the pencil, and only the part you highlight.
  • The internet is used only for cloud speech, which is off unless you turn it on and sign in with Google.

What the app reads, and why

Zypnik shows a floating pencil. When you swipe or circle part of your screen with it, the app finds links, phone numbers, email addresses, coupon codes and QR codes in that part, so you can open, call, write or copy them.

To do this, the app uses Android's Accessibility Service:

  • At the moment you swipe, it reads the text of the area you highlighted.
  • When you tap the pencil, it takes one still picture of the screen so the screen can pause while you highlight (useful for videos). If the highlighted area contains a picture (for example words inside an image or video, or a QR code), the highlighted part of that still is read on your phone using on-device recognition (Google ML Kit, bundled inside the app). The picture is deleted when you're done.
  • If you share a screenshot to Zypnik, or pick Zypnik from the text-selection menu, that picture or text is read the same way, on your phone, and not stored.
  • It also notes which app is on screen and whether a keyboard is open, only to show the pencil in the apps you chose and while you type.
  • Speak: when you use Speak, the microphone is used until you tap to stop. Your speech is turned into text by Android's speech recognition service on your phone (provided by your phone's maker or Google). Zypnik then types the text into the box you were writing in. With on-phone speech, Zypnik does not record, store or send your voice.
  • Cloud speech (optional): if you choose cloud speech and sign in with Google, the app records what you say while you are speaking and sends that recording to our server (Supabase, India region). The server passes it to a speech-to-text provider (Groq or OpenAI) and an AI clean-up step, and returns the text. We do not keep the recording or the text. We store your Google account email, an account ID and how many seconds of cloud speech you used each month, to give you your free minutes. Groq and OpenAI process the audio under their API terms, which do not use it to train their models.

The app never reads anything between swipes, never records your screen, never taps or changes anything in other apps except typing your spoken words into the box you chose, and never reads passwords or other fields you have not highlighted.

What is stored

Your settings, on your phone, and for cloud speech your sign-in and monthly usage (above). Settings: the apps you chose, which side the pencil sits on, and whether links open straight away. Nothing you highlight is saved. Uninstalling the app removes the settings.

What is shared

Nothing you highlight. There are no analytics and no advertising. If you use cloud speech, your recording goes to our speech providers only to be turned into text (see above). You can sign out in the app, and email us to delete your account and usage record. When you tap an action, the app hands the link, number or email to the app you choose on your phone (for example your browser, dialer, email app or WhatsApp). What that app does is governed by its own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
