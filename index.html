How it works

One stroke turns frozen screen text into action.

Apps are full of text you can't tap or copy: numbers in a chat, codes in an image, links in a video. Zypnik makes all of it live.

1

Tap the bubble

A small Zypnik bubble sits quietly at the edge of your screen. Tap it and the screen pauses, even on a playing video.

2

Swipe or circle

Run your finger across what you want, like a highlighter. Zypnik reads it on your phone, including text inside pictures.

3

Act in one tap

Open the link, call the number, copy the code, map the address or save the contact. Done, without switching apps.

Zypnik recognises what you swipe and offers the right action for it.

Speak

Talk instead of typing, in any app.

When you're typing, the bubble becomes a mic. Tap it, speak naturally, take your time, and tap again. Zypnik cleans up your words with AI and types them into the box you were writing in.

  • Pauses don't cut you off. It keeps listening until you tap.
  • Clean, punctuated text. Filler words and false starts are removed.
  • English and Hindi. Works in WhatsApp, Gmail, Slack and more.
  • 100 free cloud minutes a month, with on-phone speech always free.

Privacy first

Zypnik reads only what you swipe, only when you swipe.

  • On your phone. Everything you highlight is read on the device and never uploaded.
  • Nothing stored. The paused screen picture is deleted as soon as you're done.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data sales. Ever.
  • Cloud speech is opt-in. Only if you turn it on and sign in; recordings are not kept.

FAQ

Questions

Why does Zypnik need Accessibility permission?

Android only lets an app read text on your screen and show a bubble over other apps through its Accessibility feature. Zypnik uses it only at the moment you swipe, and only for the area you highlight. It never taps or changes anything in other apps.

Does it work in every app?

Yes. Zypnik works over any app, and you can choose the apps where the bubble appears. For pictures and videos it reads the paused screen, so it even works where text can't normally be selected.

Is it free?

Yes. Swiping, copying and on-phone voice typing are free. Cloud Speak includes 100 free minutes every month.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Zypnik is Android-first because iPhones don't allow a bubble over other apps.
